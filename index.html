🟢 Sustained, structural deflation

Tur Dal fell in every single tender round — six consecutive declines, from ₹1,70,490 to ₹84,830/MT. A 50.2% reduction over 16 months with no reversals.

🟠 Commodity pivot: Tur Dal → CYL

Tur Dal dominates 2024–2025 (₹3,144 Cr). From 2026 onwards CYL takes over completely — 115,000 MT versus zero Tur Dal. A deliberate portfolio shift.

🔵 Order sizes scaling up

Aug 2024 round = 40,000 MT across 3 suppliers. Jun 2026 = 75,000 MT across 8 suppliers. Spell structure changed from 3-spell to 4-spell as volumes grew.

🟣 Vendor base broadens, still top-heavy

21 suppliers, but top 5 hold 55.1% and top 10 hold 82.8%. HHI ~854 = moderately concentrated.

🟢 Repeat winners signal reliability

SKS (5 rounds, 75,000 MT), TATA (4 rounds, 58,000 MT), Arunnachala (4 rounds, 46,500 MT) win repeatedly across both commodities.

🟠 Spell structure adds WC pressure

Large orders split into 3–4 spells, each with its own Security Deposit + agreement. A 15,000 MT order locks ~₹5.84 Cr sequentially.
