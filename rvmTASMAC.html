7. RCF Simulator

The Reverse Collection Fee is the bidding parameter. Positive = you pay TASMAC. Negative = TASMAC pays you. Zero = no cash changes hands.

Key insight: The consumer always receives ₹10 per bottle from the Incentive Account, regardless of the RCF. The RCF is a separate settlement between TASMAC and the operator. A positive RCF means the operator is willing to pay for the right to operate. A negative RCF means the operator needs a subsidy.
